Who this is for

Built for a week that's already full

Most programs are written for someone whose only job is training. You've got a job, a commute and people who need you. That's not an excuse - it's the brief.

  1. Stress is stress. A hard week at work and a hard week in the gym come out of the same tank. Your program is built with that in mind, so a rough patch doesn't turn into an injury or a month off.
  2. Long term, not a six-week challenge. Blocks build on each other. The goal is that you're stronger and fitter in two years, not wrecked in six weeks.
  3. A daily check-in that actually gets read. Sleep, soreness and energy take ten seconds to log. I see it, and I adjust when the pattern says you need it.
  4. Sessions you can finish. Clear sets, reps and loads on your phone, a built-in timer, and your numbers worked out from your maxes. Turn up, follow it, go to work.

Questions

Before you sign up

What happens after I pay?

You'll get an email asking you to confirm your address. Tap the link, log in at this site with the email and password you just chose, and your account is already active. Add the app to your home screen from your browser's share menu so it opens like any other app.

How does billing work?

Your card is charged weekly, in Australian dollars, through Stripe. I never see or store your card details. You'll get a receipt by email each week.

How do I cancel?

Open the app, scroll to the bottom and tap Manage billing. You can cancel, change card or see past receipts there. There's no lock-in contract and no exit fee.

What's the difference between the plans?

Programming gets you the full program in the app, check-ins, progress tracking and leaderboards. Coaching adds me: video breakdowns of your lifts within 24 hours, chat, and a monthly check-in call.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Send me a message in the app and I'll move you across. The change shows on your next weekly bill.

Is there a phone app?

Tribe runs in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Once it's on your home screen it opens full screen, sends notifications and keeps working when the gym has no signal.

I train with you already. Do I need to pay here?

No. Log in with the email I have for you and your program is waiting. This page is for new athletes.
