Why Tribe

Every session has a job

More isn't better. Better is better. Every program is periodised across the whole year, so you build, peak and recover on purpose instead of redlining every day.

  1. No junk volume. If a session doesn't move you towards the goal, it isn't in the program. You train hard, then you recover so the hard work counts.
  2. Planned across the year. CrossFit Tribe runs an off-season block, then builds into the Open. You always know what the current block is for.
  3. Life counts. A hard week at work and a hard week in the gym come out of the same tank. Your daily check-in tells me when to push and when to pull back.
  4. Sessions you can finish. Clear sets, reps and loads on your phone, your numbers worked out from your maxes, and a built-in timer.

Questions

Before you sign up

Which program is right for me?

If you do CrossFit and want to get better at it, or you're competing, go CrossFit Tribe. If your goal is how you look - building muscle or losing fat - go Body Transformation. If you train at home or on open gym and want the same sessions my classes do, go Tribe Affiliate. Not sure? Message me and I'll point you the right way.

I'm competing. Which plan do I need?

CrossFit Tribe on the Coaching plan. I'll plan around your season - the Open, qualifiers, your next comp - and you get video breakdowns and a monthly call.

When does my program start?

Body Transformation starts at week 1 on the Monday of the week you join, so everyone starts at the beginning. CrossFit Tribe and Tribe Affiliate run year round, so you join this week's sessions with everyone else.

I run a gym. Can I use the Tribe class program?

Yes. Tap Enquire under Tribe Affiliate, tell me about your gym, and I'll send you the details and price.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get an email asking you to confirm your address. Tap the link, log in at this site with the email and password you just chose, and your account is already active. Add the app to your home screen from your browser's share menu so it opens like any other app.

How does billing work?

Your card is charged weekly, in Australian dollars, through Stripe. I never see or store your card details. You'll get a receipt by email each week.

How do I cancel?

Open the app, scroll to the bottom and tap Manage billing. You can cancel, change card or see past receipts there. There's no lock-in contract and no exit fee.

Can I switch programs or plans later?

Yes. Send me a message in the app and I'll move you across. The change shows on your next weekly bill.

Is there a phone app?

Tribe runs in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Once it's on your home screen it opens full screen, sends notifications and keeps working when the gym has no signal.

I train with you already. Do I need to pay here?

No. Log in with the email I have for you and your program is waiting. This page is for new athletes.
